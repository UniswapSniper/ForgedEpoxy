Our Installation Process

A proven 4-step process ensuring flawless results every single time.

1
🔧

Surface Preparation

We thoroughly diamond grind your concrete, removing old coatings and creating the perfect profile for adhesion. All cracks and damage are repaired.

2
🎨

Base Coat Application

Our premium base coat is expertly applied at the ideal thickness, ensuring maximum durability and the perfect foundation for your chosen finish.

3
✨

Flake Broadcasting

Decorative flakes are broadcasted to 100% coverage, creating your desired aesthetic. After curing, excess flake is removed for a smooth finish.

4
💎

Clear Top Coat

The final UV-stable polyaspartic top coat is applied, sealing everything in a glossy, chemical-resistant finish that's ready to use in 24 hours.
